Space Emperors · Shipyard

Mixed ship batches as widgets

Each Shipyard is one message whose bubble runs a widget. The draft lives in the widget, so dragging and tapping edits nothing until the player orders. The order goes to the bot as one request, the bot edits the widget's state, and the same message then counts down the batch, line by line, without further edits.

  • Drag a slider or tap the steps, then order. The mock bot answers like the real one would.
  • Move the clock to finish ships; the bot edits the widget and posts the notification when the batch ends.
  • ➕ Add ships switches the same message back to the composer for the running batch.
  • The buttons under each widget stay inline buttons: typed names and navigation do not belong in a widget. Button layouts A–G
